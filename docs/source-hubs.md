# Organological and collection source hubs

Museum catalogues can broaden the ontology beyond the best-indexed modal-theory literatures.
They expose names, attributions, construction, morphology, materials, makers, acquisition
histories, and sometimes recordings or tuning information.

Useful discovery layers include MIMO/Europeana, Horniman Museum and Gardens, the Metropolitan
Museum of Art musical-instrument collection, and Smithsonian/Folkways resources.

## Epistemic rule

`museum_collection_record` is a provenance type, not a truth status.

It may strongly evidence object morphology, material, dimensions, physical tuning mechanisms,
and collection history. It may weakly or not at all evidence locally recognized pitch
categories, normative tuning, repertorial function, continuity, or cultural meaning.

Historical museum naming can itself be data. Preserve catalogue labels, but do not silently
promote broad collector categories into preferred ontology concepts.
