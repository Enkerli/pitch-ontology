# How does speech interact with pitch?

Pitch organization can emerge through relations among lexical tone, prosody,
recitation, cantillation, heightened speech, and song.

Useful relations:
- `CONSTRAINS`
- `SHAPED_BY`
- `REPRESENTS`
- `ILLUMINATES_ASPECT_OF`

Contour may be more stable or culturally consequential than interval size, making it
a first-class object rather than secondary metadata.
