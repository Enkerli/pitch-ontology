Pitch Ontology

Preliminary work towards a situated, relational vocabulary for pitch — how people and technologies perceive, categorize, organize, tune, perform, represent and theorize it, with no tradition as the unmarked default. Not a catalogue of “the scales of the world,” and not yet an ontology.
